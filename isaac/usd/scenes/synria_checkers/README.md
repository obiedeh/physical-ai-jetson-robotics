# Synria + Checkers Tabletop Training Scene

Training scene for the Synria 6DOF arm playing American checkers / English draughts: arm mounted on a table with an 8 × 8 board and 24 discs staged in the standard opening position.

## Layout

- Floor, table (1.20 × 0.80 × 0.75 m), Synria arm — shared scaffolding via `isaac/scripts/_synria_scene_common.py`
- Board: 8 × 8 squares, 0.045 m per square (0.36 m playing area), 0.02 m border → 0.40 m total board, centered at (x = 0.10, y = 0). Same geometry as the chess scene.
- 24 disc pieces (12 per side) on dark squares only
  - White: dark squares on ranks 1, 2, 3
  - Black: dark squares on ranks 6, 7, 8
- Each piece is a low cylinder: 0.018 m radius, 0.008 m thick
- **Four staging zones** (board ↔ staging pick-and-place targets, one per side of the board):
  - `left` (blue, −Y of board), `right` (orange, +Y), `top` (purple, −X toward arm), `bottom` (green, +X toward operator)
  - See [docs/SYNRIA_PICK_AND_PLACE_TASK.md](../../../../docs/SYNRIA_PICK_AND_PLACE_TASK.md) for the task spec
- Overhead RGB camera (board-state view) + operator perspective camera

Units: meters, kilograms, Z-up.

## Training task

Primary task: pick a disc off the board, place it on one of the four staging zones, return it to its original dark square. Full spec in [docs/SYNRIA_PICK_AND_PLACE_TASK.md](../../../../docs/SYNRIA_PICK_AND_PLACE_TASK.md).

Initial state for this scene:
- 24 discs on dark squares of ranks 1–3 (white) and 6–8 (black)
- All four staging zones empty

## Build

The scene is generated by [`isaac/scripts/build_synria_checkers_scene.py`](../../scripts/build_synria_checkers_scene.py).

On the Linux RTX 5090 workstation:

```bash
bash scripts/linux_rtx/build_synria_checkers_scene.sh
```

The script writes `synria_checkers_v0.usda` to this folder. Commit the result after a successful build.

## Validation checklist (run once on RTX)

- [ ] Script completes without errors under Isaac Sim 5.1
- [ ] Open the resulting USD in Isaac Sim — stage loads, no missing-prim warnings
- [ ] Synria arm reference resolves and the arm appears mounted on the table top
- [ ] Confirm arm reach: every dark square is within 0.65 m of the arm base
- [ ] Confirm piece-pickup clearance: gripper open width fits 0.036 m disc diameter
- [ ] No pieces appear on light squares
- [ ] 12 + 12 = 24 pieces total, three rows on each side, alternating across files
- [ ] Overhead camera frames the full board with margin
- [ ] Physics scene present, pieces respond to gravity

## Known limitations (intentional, refine on RTX)

- Kings (crowned pieces) are not modeled at opening. The trainer / game logic crowns a disc when it reaches the far rank — could be modeled as stacking two discs or swapping the prim to a taller variant.
- Materials are flat display colors. Add wood-grain board texture and disc edge bevels in v1.
- Square coordinate labels (file a-h, rank 1-8) are encoded in prim names (`sq_a1`, etc.) but not displayed visually on the board edges.
