## A. GR00T on the Franka + Robotiq 2F-85 surrogate — CONCLUDED

The surrogate exists because the Synria gripper cannot grasp in sim (see
workstream C). It answers the mission question — can GR00T learn this
task — with real physics.

Results, all measured on the fixed harness
(`isaac/scripts/eval_gr00t_franka.py`, 900-step windows, spawn ±80 mm):

| ckpt | demos | eff. batch | steps | open-loop MAE (zeros ≈ 0.57–0.60) | closed-loop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v4 | 60 rand | 1 | 5k | 0.228 | 0/24 |
| v5 (control) | 22 fixed-spawn | 1 | 5k | 0.212 | **3 places / 32** (fixed spawn) |
| v6 | 200 rand | 1 | 5k | 0.570 (= baseline) | skipped |
| v6b | 200 rand | 1 | 15k | 0.808 (worse than baseline) | skipped |
| v6c | 200 rand | 8 (accum) | 5k | 0.0599 | **1 place / 32** (randomized) |
| v6d | 200 rand | 8 | 16k | 0.0355 | 0/40 |
| v7 | 226 (200 + 26 dwell) | 8 | 6k | — | 0/40 |

Two findings worth carrying forward:
1. **Effective batch size is decisive for diverse corpora.** At batch 1
   the model trains to (or past) the trivial baseline; gradient
   accumulation to batch 8 — identical VRAM — produced the best
   imitation of the project. Any future run must use batch ≥ 8.
2. **Fixed-spawn control (v5) produced the project's first GR00T task
   completions**, proving the policy learns the *skill* but not the
   *visual retargeting*.

Ended at a documented blocker (`docs/experiment_ledger.md`, "VERIFIED
BLOCKER"): with the DiT frozen (forced by 32 GB VRAM; NVIDIA's full
fine-tune floor is 40 GB), the policy cannot produce state-conditioned

(the bundle is rebuilt in one command — see `docs/ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.md`).

## C. Synria gripper audit + repair — OPEN, training BLOCKED

Read this section with the ordering in mind: it contains two
self-corrections. The final state is at the end.

### Firmly measured
- **Cup**: 40.0 mm diameter x 50 mm, primitive cylinder, collision =
  visual, no scale, no taper.
- **Gripper**: prismatic fingers, limits [0, 25] / [-25, 0] mm confirmed
  in-sim; pad faces +/-25.0 mm from the centreline -> **50.0 mm usable
  opening**; pads 43.3 x 60 mm, ~1800 mm^2 face; no mimic joints, no
  scale, no transmission. Against the 40 mm cup that is 5 mm per side
  (marginal-acceptable). Recommended object **25-30 mm** (suggest
  28 x 60 mm); keep the 40 mm cup only as a stress case.
- **Finger travel is healthy**: with the base raised clear of the floor,
  the production asset completes 25.00 mm per finger for 10 open/close
  cycles.
- **Pad colliders are live**: the spawned articulation reports
  `pad_collider ... enabled=True`; the finger **mesh** colliders report
  `enabled=False` (dead, but redundant).
- **Contact works** once objects are placed at the true pad centre:
  fingers stall at 0-6 mm travel instead of closing through.
- **Arm links still have no collision geometry** — a real, unfixed
  defect (only 4 collision prims exist in the whole robot, all on the
  fingers).

### Two conclusions this session had to withdraw
1. *"Static phantom pad colliders block travel at 0.3-0.4 mm"* -
   **wrong**. The pads are correctly parented to the moving finger
   links. The stalls were the arm sagging onto the floor because the
   test harness used the URDF's real gains (5 Nm, stiffness 80), which
   cannot hold the commanded pose against gravity.
2. *"No finger collider contacts dynamic bodies"* - **wrong**. The pad
   collider spans about -15 mm to +58 mm in z *relative to the finger
   frame* (the finger extends diagonally). Tests placed objects at
   "frame - 30 mm", below the collider entirely, so nothing was ever
   between the pads.

Both corrections are recorded with their evidence in
`reports/synria_grasp_audit.md` (AMENDMENT and SECOND AMENDMENT); no
wrong conclusion was deleted.

Four phases settled, one open, four untouched. Note that the `0/12`
(blocks) and `0/10` (early scripted grasps) figures are *failed
measurements*, not partial progress.

### What is actually left to do
- **Phase 5 fixture**: widths still cannot be measured reliably because
  the arm drifts under contact load (the object's offset from the pads
  walked from -0.5 mm to -21 mm in x across 12 trials). Build a rigid
  fixture that fixes the object pose relative to the *pads* and
  re-measures immediately before the close. This is a test-harness job,
  not an asset edit.
- **Audit grasp poses**: the most likely control-side bug is that grasp
  heights target the finger **frame** instead of the **pad centre** -
  roughly a 20-30 mm error, and the fingers extend diagonally. Check
  this in both the RL environment and the GR00T scripts before blaming
  physics again.
- **Add arm-link collision geometry** (boxes/convex hulls from the
  visual meshes).
- Then Phases 6-9: environment wiring with a startup assert, the 28 mm
  validation object, the 10-trial grasp gate, and the launcher guard.

### Gate
`isaac/scripts/synria_grasp_feasibility.py` is the reproducible gate.
Modes: default scripted trials, `--travel_test`, `--block_test`,
`--hover_test`; isolation switches `--free_air`, `--no_world`,
`--no_self_collision`, `--raise_base`, and
`--usd {production,g30,nopad,padfix,padfix_l,padfix2,repaired}`.
Exit codes: 0 pass, 3 travel fail, 4 block fail.

**Acceptance criterion (from the brief): at least 9 successful grasp
trials out of 10**, with no phantom collision, no mesh penetration, no
unexplained finger jam, and no false success from the object resting on
the ground. The "9/10" is a pass threshold, not a progress count — the
gate has never yet been run to completion. The 40 mm cup is reported
separately as a marginal stress case and is not required to pass.

**Synria policy training stays blocked** until the gate passes, and the
training launchers should be wired to refuse to start until it does
(Phase 7, not yet done).

### Standing lesson
Two false "the asset is broken" verdicts came from this session's own
test harness, not the asset. Before concluding that a simulation asset
is defective, verify the test rig: that the robot holds its pose, and
that the object is actually inside the collider you think it is.

## Operating notes for whoever picks this up

- Kill the *python child* by script name, then verify with `nvidia-smi`;
  bash wrappers survive `pkill` on the parent and orphans have exhausted
  the GPU before.
- Isaac runs hang after printing their result during env teardown; the
  recorder now hard-exits on purpose. Budget for this in any driver.
- Never `pip install` into `~/.venv/isaacsim5` without `--no-deps` and a
  pin check — a dependency once replaced torch with a cu126 build and
  broke Blackwell kernels.
- `HF_TOKEN` must be exported explicitly for model loads; the cached
  token file is not picked up in-process.
- When a test "passes", check the criterion before believing it: two
  false positives in this session (a hover test counting a fall to the
  ground as a hold, and pinned test objects that could not receive
  contact impulses) were caught only by re-reading the raw numbers.
