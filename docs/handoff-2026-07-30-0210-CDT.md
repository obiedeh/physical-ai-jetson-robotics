## 1. THE GOAL

Pick up the Ludo cup, lift it, move it to a defined target, place it
**upright**, release it, and return the arm safely to its reset pose.

**Acceptance criteria** (user-specified): >= 90% grasp-and-lift; >= 80%
full pick-and-place; success from multiple cup positions and arm poses;
no safety violations (collision checks, joint/velocity/effort limits,
workspace bounds, gripper-force limits were never disabled at any point).

**Operating protocol** (user-specified): fixed-conditions evaluation ->
single largest failure -> smallest change -> bounded experiment ->
same-conditions eval -> keep/revise/revert -> ledger; stop-and-diagnose
after 5 consecutive non-improvements; no long runs without demonstrated
short-run improvement.

## 3. WHAT WAS ACHIEVED (chronologically)

1. Revived the six-week-dead training pipeline (rsl-rl pin, configclass
   fixes, STOP-handler, exit-code discipline).
2. Fixed a generation of reward exploits (sign inversions, hover farm,
   shuffleboard pushing, pendulum farm, latch farm, drop-and-park,
   bombing runs) — the reward economy is now positive-income and
   farm-audited.
3. Re-imported the arm asset (v1 USD carried a different robot's
   collision payload); box finger pads + friction material.
4. Built curriculum machinery (pregrasp, surface-pick, docked/
   carry-gradient, return-start staged slices) with reset-transient
   pinning; taught grasp, hold, release, and return in sequence.
5. **Made the destination observable** (it was a category label; the
   policy literally could not see where to go) — transformed carry
   learning.
6. Diagnosed and routed around the **gripper/table contact-fidelity
   wall** (measured: fingers jam at 10 mm width against open commands
   under table-crush; cup slips in lateral carries at 5 N and 20 N)
   via **kinematic attach-on-grasp** (pick stays physical, release
   decision stays learned; standard sim scaffold, to be retired by the
   gripper-fidelity tracks).
7. Instrumented everything: Funnel/* per-stage training telemetry and
   the fixed per-stage eval harness; feasible-spawn audit (half of all
   spawns had been geometrically impossible; grasp was 84% where
   physics allowed).
8. Met **acceptance criterion #1**: grasp-and-lift 0.909/0.909 (E15).
9. Aligned the task with the goal spec: defined placement target
   (observable), upright gate, graduated tolerances.
10. Diagnosed the final structural blocker — **a clipped Gaussian
    action head cannot learn a binary gripper decision** — and fixed it
    with Isaac Lab's BinaryJointPositionAction (E22), validating
    immediately with 5x-record placements and a 100% place->return
    conversion.
