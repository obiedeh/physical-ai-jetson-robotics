## 5. E32–E33: the scripted expert

**E32 sweep**: grasping is trivially solved — 6/6 upright at EVERY height
46→37 mm, pitch 0° and 45° (135° cannot acquire). Placement failed at
every setting; eliminated as causes: grasp height, pitch, speed (8×),
grip force (5 N vs 40 N identical), **cup diameter** (the audit's
recommended 28 mm object placed WORSE than the 40 mm cup — rec #5
refuted), lift dynamics (20° lift slip fully recovers on table contact).

**Operator visual diagnosis** (minutes of watching vs hours of
instrumentation): the arm moves while the gripper is still partially
closed on the cup. Measured confirmation: commanded-open fingers stall at
**38.3 mm gap — narrower than the 40 mm cup** — because the
cup-referenced set-down parks the pad bottoms at −0.1 mm ON the table,
and opening then means sliding pads laterally against table friction with
a 5 N actuator. Trace: 0.8° after release → 25° after home.

**E33 loop** (goal ≥11/12, one change per iteration):

| iter | change | PLACED |
|---|---|---|
| base | verified wait + home | 6/12 |
| 1 | operator sequence (open-while-descending, pad-tip stop, straight-up) | 3/12 — release+exit PERFECT; homing hit the cup |
| 2 | verified absolute climb, homing gated | 4/12 — home pose descends over the cup |
| 3 | ordered homing (yaw out of sector at altitude, then descend) | **12/12, 0 tipped** |

**The E33 cycle** (in `isaac/scripts/sweep_grasp_geometry.py`, judged
after full homing, 25 ± 5 mm, ≤15°, across ±24 mm spawn offsets):
grasp z=40 → lift → transport (yaw) → descend easing jaws ~55% (cup
seats under its own weight) → stop at pad-tip table contact (+1 mm) →
verified full open (gap ≥ 44 mm) → straight up, no lateral → yaw home at
altitude → descend to rest.

**Design rules extracted** (these are the research findings):
1. Never move the arm while the cup is constrained by the jaws.
2. Never let the pads bear on the table — a 5 N finger cannot slide
   loaded pads (the 36.1 mm underhang decides everything near a surface).
3. Never sweep the hand through the object's airspace — exit straight
   up; re-path homing around the placement, not through it.
4. Contact events beat computed heights (pad-tip touch as the stop
   signal absorbs the ~5.8 mm in-jaw slip that makes cup- and
   pad-referenced set-downs disagree).

## 6. Methodology results (for the write-up)

- **Nine harness bugs** in this line alone; the ninth (inverted release
  polarity: 0.025 is CLOSED) was the one that mattered most. Every bug
  was found by checking a number against what it physically had to be —
  none by reading code.
- **A false 6/6** was caught only by retract-then-judge. Any placement
  metric that judges before the hand is gone is wrong.
- **OR-latched stage flags inflate conditionals** (0.698 → 0.389). Emit
  one row per episode; compute conditionals offline.
- **Pre-registration with a primary metric** rejected two would-be
  positives that secondary metrics would have "confirmed".
- **A visualizer beats instrumentation for mechanism discovery** — two
  for two this session. Instrument to verify, watch to discover.
- Failure-mode mixes are budget-dependent (33% vs 89% tipping at 5k vs
  25k): diagnose at the budget you intend to treat.
